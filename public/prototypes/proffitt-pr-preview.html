Chapter One — Hello

Hello, We’re Proffitt PR

We’re a savvy, boutique consulting firm specializing in making you and your brand look good. Public relations, strategic marketing, social media management, advertising, community relations, branding and identity, digital marketing, sponsorships — every tool in the toolbox, wielded with Southern hospitality and a work ethic our mamas would brag about.

Over the past decade we’ve helped more than 200 local, national, and international brands leave with greater visibility along the Emerald Coast and a lot more excitement for what they do. Our community has noticed: they’ve repeatedly named us the best public relations, event planning, and advertising firm around, and we couldn’t be more grateful.

We didn’t start in a boardroom. We started at coffee shop tables, showing up to every networking event on the coast until everybody knew our name. Fourteen years later, we still show up — just with better shoes.

Filed Under: Festivals

The Festival That Sells Out the Parking Lot

Every fall, The Market Shops’ main lawn fills with tents, tasting cups, and a very happy crowd — that’s the Bloody Mary Festival, and we’ve run point on it for years. Media alerts, sponsor wrangling, social buzz, day-of coordination: the whole production, down to the celery garnish. It’s become one of the coast’s can’t-miss fall traditions, and the phones at the surrounding restaurants don’t stop ringing after.

“The lawn was packed by noon.”

— Festival regular, probably holding a tasting cup

Filed Under: Community

A Night Out That Gives Back

In 2011 Jessica gathered a few dozen local women for camaraderie and called it Girls’ Night Out. It grew into hundreds of women raising thousands of dollars for local charities — one holiday soiree at Marlin Grill raised $4,682.38 for breast cancer awareness. We plan it, promote it, and pour the wine. Some clients hire us for reach; this one’s for the soul.

Filed Under: Grand Openings

Open for Business, Loudly

Amazing Lash Studio’s Destin debut. Bracken Law’s second office on 30A. Clean Juice landing at The Market Shops with 20+ new jobs. Our grand openings come with ribbon cuttings, press, crowds, and lines out the door — because “now open” should feel like an event, not a footnote.

Chapter Five — The Firm

The Woman Behind the Hustle

In 2011, Jessica Proffitt Bracken decided to venture out on her own. She started by attending every networking event on the Emerald Coast she could find. Five years later, she hosted a grand opening at The Market Shops with 300 friends, three chambers of commerce, client-supplied cocktails, a photo booth, and selfie sticks for the first hundred guests — because of course there were selfie sticks.

She learned traditional PR at the elbow of Kay Phelan, the matriarch of Emerald Coast PR, and she runs on a motto her dad instilled early: persistence removes resistance. In 2022, 850 Business Magazine gave her the Pinnacle Award. She’s still trying to resurrect Girls’ Night Out. Stay tuned, ladies.
