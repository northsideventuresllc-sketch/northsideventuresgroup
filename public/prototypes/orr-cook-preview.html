FLIP ONE — 1951

A LAWYER HANGS HIS SHINGLE

Carl Dawson helps his first client in Jacksonville, Florida — a small Southern town about a third the size it is today. He builds a reputation the old way: integrity, professionalism, and showing up. As Jacksonville grows into a major city, the firm keeps pace.

FLIP TWO — 1953

DAWSON FLIPS A COIN WITH LUKE GALANT

Two lawyers. One coin. It lands, and the partnership is set — on the ground where the firm's downtown Jacksonville office still stands today. Some firms are founded on paperwork. This one was founded on a toss.

FLIP THREE — THE NAMING

ORR AND COOK STAND WITH CARL

Decades later, Michael Orr and Kevin Cook stand beside Carl Dawson as he flips the coin one more time — to name the firm. It lands: Orr | Cook. Storied roots, a team at the top of their game, and a name decided the way this firm decides everything: all in.

THE COIN LANDED. WE NEVER STOPPED BETTING ON OUR CLIENTS.

High-stakes deals, disputes, and litigation — handled like a client's first and last choice. That's the firm the coin built.
