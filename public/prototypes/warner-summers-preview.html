The Firm Today

A New Era, Same Drafting Table.

Founded in 1964, Warner Summers is a boutique, full-service architecture and interior design firm — which means agile, responsive, and experienced in all aspects of design. Excellent service is the point: it is how the firm has formed partnerships that have lasted decades.

In 2020, partner Dana Ladd led the buyout that made Warner Summers a certified woman-owned small business. Six decades of institutional knowledge — the relationships, the details, the way Atlanta gets built — now carried forward under new leadership.

Dana Ladd, AIA, LEED AP

Partner · Director of Architecture & Business Development

Dana leads the staff in the design of large commercial and medical building types, and heads up business development — finding new opportunities, knowing the players, and setting the firm's course. A leader in CREW Atlanta and a frequent speaker around the city. Clemson and Georgia Tech graduate.
