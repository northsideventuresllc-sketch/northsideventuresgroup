The Record

The Record, Not the Claim

Any firm can say "fiduciary." Westgate has the dates. Four entries, four decades, one through-line: the client's interest first, with or without a law requiring it.

1986

Founded in Tacoma

Westgate Capital Consultants opens in the Puget Sound area as an independent firm — no proprietary products, no house mandates.

2009

The AIF Designation

An advisor earns the Accredited Investment Fiduciary mark — one of two in Tacoma, 73 in Washington. Sought out, not imposed.

2011

The Standard Spreads

Ian Hartley earns his AIF designation. The fiduciary standard becomes the firm's operating system, not one advisor's credential.

2025

Joins HUB International

Becomes HUB Retirement and Wealth Management — University Place. Four decades of local trust, now with national resources behind it.

Along the way: one of the first firms in the region to offer individual account-based retirement plans — and a standing invitation to ask how the firm is paid, answered plainly.
