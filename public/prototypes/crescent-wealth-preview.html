Nº 02Independence

The Signature Question

Who Does Your Advisor Work For?

Most firms can't answer that cleanly. Open each line of the ledger and compare — the big brokerage on the left, Crescent on the right.

The Big Brokerage
Crescent

Large firms answer to shareholders and sales targets. Crescent is independent and employee-owned — no parent company, no quotas, no house products to push. Our only constituency sits across the desk.

Commissions and product markups can quietly reward selling over advising. Crescent works on a transparent, fee-based footing — when we recommend something, it's because it fits your plan, not because it pays us more.

Captive advisors often choose from their own firm's shelf. Crescent runs an open-architecture platform — ETFs to private equity, any manager, any vehicle — with institutional-level due diligence and reporting that aggregates everything you own into one view.

We're fiduciaries — legally bound to act in your best interest, in an unbiased and conflict-free manner. Not as a marketing line. As the structure of the firm.

Tim Wyrobek and Jeff Taylor ran an asset-protection consultancy since 2003. Their own clients asked them to handle investing and planning with the same care — so they built the advisory firm their clients designed.

Illustrative comparison. Firms differ; the point here is structural — independence you can verify, not independence you have to take on faith.

How We Work

Disciplined, Then Personal

Institutional rigor underneath; a family-office conversation on top.

Pillar I

Customization

Risk profiles and manager selection built around each client — never a model portfolio with your name on it.

Pillar II

Institutional Due Diligence

Every manager, security, and allocation passes an institutional-grade review before it touches your money.

Pillar III

Continuous monitoring, quarterly reporting, and at least one full review with you every year — more when life changes.

Nº 05The Firm

Our Founding

Asked Into Existence

Most firms are founded to find clients. Crescent was founded because it already had them.

2003

The Consultancy

Tim Wyrobek and Jeff Taylor found Crescent Wealth Management, a high-touch asset-protection consultancy. Clients trust them with the most sensitive part of their wealth: keeping it safe.

2016

The Request

Those same clients ask Wyrobek and Taylor to handle their investing and financial planning with the same comprehensive care. Crescent Wealth Advisory is launched — at their clients' direct request.

2017

The Platform

Crescent joins the Dynasty Financial Partners network, gaining institutional custody, reporting, and manager research — while staying independent and employee-owned.

Today

Two Offices, One Ledger

Winter Park, Florida and Baton Rouge, Louisiana. A ten-person team serving individuals, families, and family offices across the Southeast — still answering only to clients.
