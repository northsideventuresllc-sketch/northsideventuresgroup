The Method

Every Search Is a Production

Our process, in five phases. The names are ours; the discipline is thirty-plus years old.

PHASE 01
The ScriptTargeted Research

We work with your board and staff to define the qualitative and quantitative attributes the role demands — the ideal candidate profile, selection criteria, and what success looks like in year one.

PHASE 02
CastingCandidate Identification & Outreach

We scour the universes of technology, entertainment, media, academia, the arts, and finance — reaching the important players and rising stars, plus the analysts, journalists, and thought leaders around them. Passive candidates, globally.

PHASE 03
Screen TestsCandidate Evaluation

Comprehensive assessment for cultural and technical fit, backed by certified assessment tools. We test for the role as it will actually be lived, not as it reads on paper.

PHASE 04
The PremiereClient Presentation

A curated slate, presented with the context to decide well: background, assessment, references, and our read on fit — the full picture, not a stack of résumés.

PHASE 05
Closing CreditsNegotiations & Close

We stay in the room through offer, negotiation, and acceptance — advising on compensation and retention so the placement holds past year one.
