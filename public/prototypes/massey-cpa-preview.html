What We Do — In Plain Words

Start With Your Worry. We'll Take It From There.

Every service below started as a question a real client asked us. Find yours.

Tax Problem Resolution

What If I Haven't Filed in Years?

Then you're our favorite kind of client — because the relief is enormous. We file the back returns, deal with the IRS and the state directly, and negotiate what you owe down to what's fair: offers in compromise, payment plans, penalty relief. You never have to talk to the IRS yourself.

Includes: back tax returns · audit defense · liens & levies · payroll tax problems · currently-not-collectible status

Tax Planning

Am I Paying More Tax Than I Should?

Probably. Most people are. We meet with you through the year — not just in April — and look for every legal way to keep your money: entity choice, retirement planning, cost segregation on real estate, R&D credits, charitable strategies, 1031 exchanges. Planning is where the real savings live.

Includes: year-round strategy meetings · entity choice · retirement & estate coordination · real estate tax strategy

Tax Preparation

Just the Returns, Done Right

Individual 1040s, partnerships, S corporations, C corporations, nonprofits, trusts, estates — prepared accurately, filed on time, with every deduction and credit you qualify for. And we actually explain what we did, in words you'd use.

Includes: federal & all state returns · quarterly estimates · e-filing · IRS correspondence handled

Bookkeeping & QuickBooks

Who Handles the Monthly Mess?

We do. Clean books, done on time, in QuickBooks Online set up the right way from day one. Financial statements you can actually read, budgets and forecasts, AP/AR handled. You run the business; we keep the numbers honest.

Includes: monthly bookkeeping · QuickBooks setup & training · financial statements · budgeting & forecasting

Business Formation

Starting Something New?

The entity you pick on day one decides how much tax you pay for years. We walk you through sole proprietorship vs. LLC vs. S corporation in plain English, set it up right, and stay with you as it grows. The cheapest tax decision you'll ever make is the first one.

Includes: entity selection · formation paperwork · startup tax calendar · first-year planning

Fractional CFO

Need a Finance Brain Without the Hire?

A full-time CFO is overkill; no CFO at all is risky. We sit in the middle — cash-flow planning, pricing decisions, growth strategy, someone to call before the big moves. Senior financial thinking, sized for a small business budget.

Includes: cash-flow forecasting · KPI dashboards · growth planning · on-call advice
