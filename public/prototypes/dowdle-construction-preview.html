Stop 04 — How We Work

The Way We Do Business

Four things you can count on. None of them are slogans — they're how the jobs actually run.

Open-Book, Negotiated

Much of our work is negotiated, not bid. We show our math. Owners see costs as they develop and make decisions with real numbers, not allowances and change orders.

Design-Assist From Day One

We start at schematic drawings, partnering with owners and architects early so the vision stays on budget and on track before a single wall comes down.

Compressed Schedules

"We don't like to say that can't be done." Fast-track delivery is our native language — restaurants that have to open, markets that have to launch, timelines other contractors won't touch.

Built Green

LEED-trained project managers, solar and skylight installations, construction waste minimized and recycled. Environmental responsibility is part of the bid, not an upgrade.
