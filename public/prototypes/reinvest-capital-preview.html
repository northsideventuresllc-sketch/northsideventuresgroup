Our Services

Every Way a Deal Gets Done

Six advisory practices, one franchise-only lens. Whether you're buying, selling, recapitalizing, or restructuring, the team that only does franchise deals sees angles generalists miss.

  1. 01

    Sell Side M&A

    Full sale processes tailored to franchise systems — positioning multi-unit portfolios, running competitive bids, and maximizing value whether you're an owner-operator, family office, or stakeholder group.

  2. 02

    Buy Side M&A

    Targeted acquisition searches inside franchise systems: identifying operators ready to sell, underwriting unit-level economics, and structuring offers franchisors will approve.

  3. 03

    Opinions of Value

    Independent, defensible valuations grounded in real franchise transaction comps — for sales, buyouts, partner disputes, and estate planning.

  4. 04

    Recapitalization

    Bringing in growth capital or taking chips off the table without giving up the business — structured around how franchise cash flows actually work.

  5. 05

    Restructuring

    Navigating distressed franchise situations: 363 sales, lender negotiations, and court-supervised processes. This is where the 90-unit Burger King auction was won.

  6. 06

    Real Estate

    Sale-leasebacks and real-estate strategy for franchise operators sitting on valuable dirt — freeing capital trapped in the property beneath the business.

Burger King Restaurants

90Restaurants Sold

A multi-unit portfolio of 90 Burger King restaurants across the Mid-Atlantic and Midwest moved from one ownership group to four different buyers — through a Chapter 11 363 auction. ReInvest ran the marketing and sale process: identifying bidders, working with the debtor-in-possession, the creditors' committee, and the bankruptcy court to land a stalking-horse bid, then driving the auction past it.

"The ReInvest Capital team led by David Rego managed the marketing and sale of the 90 Burger King restaurants in the Toms King Chapter 11 case where I was CRO. They did a good job identifying potential bidders and generating strong interest — we signed a stalking horse at a good price and the auction generated an even higher value."Dan Dooley — Principal and CRO

Portfolio Asset Sale

Subway Restaurants

176Restaurants Sold

176 Subway restaurants across Texas and the Mid-Atlantic transferred from two ownership groups to three buyers. The work was aggregation: rolling units owned by numerous franchisees into a single marketed portfolio, with the franchisor's development team at the table — a structure that benefited sellers and buyers alike.

"David Rego and the ReInvest Capital team did a fantastic job assisting us in selling our Subway portfolio. They helped navigate the sales process, and even helped assist after closing."Vik Agrawal — Owner
